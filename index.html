Picture Build

Slide, clear and complete the picture!

How to play

  • Drag a block to slide it. Blocks can't pass through each other or the dark wall tiles.
  • Push a block into a gate of the same color to clear it. The block has to fit inside the gate.
  • Blocks with white arrows only slide the way the arrows point.
  • Slide every picture piece (cream tiles) onto its spot in the blueprint. It locks in and becomes part of the floor.
  • Finish the picture before the timer runs out.

Design choices in this prototype

  • The board is shaped like the picture you're building. Cells outside the shape are dark wall tiles; gates sit on the shape's outline.
  • The blueprint is a line drawing on normal floor. Any block can slide over it or park on it. Only the matching piece locks there.
  • Placed pieces become part of the floor. Other blocks slide over them, so placing pieces never traps anything.
  • You win when the picture is complete. Leftover blocks clear first, then the picture lights up and comes to life (the rocket launches, the car drives off).
  • Picture pieces never leave through gates. Gates are only for blocks.
  • Pieces are cut along the grid (2×2 and 1×2 tiles), so they always fill the blueprint exactly.
  • Shuffle mixes up block colors (arrow blocks keep theirs), and only into colors whose gate the block fits.
  • Both levels were checked with a solver and can be completed. Every block fits at least one gate of its color.
  • Power-ups refill for free when empty (prototype only). The 4th slot shows a locked power-up, as in the concept.
